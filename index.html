The inquiry began with a simple claim: “It’s just a video.” Investigators obtained the original files, checked the timestamps, and immediately questioned the phrase “just.”

Probable cause

Two recordings show different looks, different lighting and the same relaxed expression. No statement has been offered to explain why the camera appears to be cooperating so enthusiastically.

Chain of custody

Still frames were recovered from the footage. The newsroom was instructed to select just three. Selection took much longer than anyone is willing to admit.

Conflict of interest

The reporter disclosed a close friendship with Aisha. Management considered assigning someone neutral, then remembered this newspaper has no management.

Expert finding

The case has one constant: Aisha looks completely untroubled by how good the evidence is.
